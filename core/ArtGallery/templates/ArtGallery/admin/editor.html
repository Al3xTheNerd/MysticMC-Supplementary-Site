Instructions

  • Only 128x128 pixel images can be uploaded. The easiest way to get the images of the artwork is with the mod Map.png on Modrinth.
  • Go to Settings>Controls>Map.png>Download Map and set whatever keybind you like, I personally use F8 (default), but whatever is most convenient for you will work.
  • Look at a map in an item frame, or hold it in your hand and press the Download Map key you set in the previous step.
  • From there, you should see a message in chat saying something along the lines of Downloaded: map_{nums}.png. This is the 128x128 copy of the map that you will be uploading to the site.
  • Repeat the previous two steps until you have downloaded a copy of each piece of the artwork you want to upload.
  • Fill out the details in the above form, Only Art Name and Server are required, however you can optionally add extra details if you would like to do so.
  • Click Choose Files, and select all of the pieces of the map you'd like to upload. They will be in Instance Folder/maps/multiplayer/{Server IP}. The easiest way to get to your Instance Folder is to go to your resource packs ingame, then click open pack folder, and go back one folder.
  • Use the Layout Builder to organize all of your map pieces into their respective places. This is a Drag and Drop system, so.. Drag things, and drop them. There are buttons available to add extra columns and rows as needed. Blank spaces are perfectly allowed as well, for those of you with weirdly shaped maparts.
  • Click Save Art, and voila! Your art is now visible for all to see!
